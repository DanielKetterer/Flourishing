Competence in managing your surroundings, making effective use of opportunities, choosing and shaping contexts

Continued development, openness to new experiences, realizing your potential over time

Warm, trusting connections with others — feeling known, cared for, and able to care in return

Sense of direction, goals that give life meaning, feeling there is a point to your present and past
